Main-thread stacks captured inside the stall
============================================

Source: Instruments "Time Profiler" recording of `auto-repro`
(`xcrun xctrace record --device '<device name>' --template 'Time Profiler'
--time-limit 70s --launch -- com.example.SpatialAudioStallRepro auto-repro`),
then

    xcrun xctrace export --input stall_profiler.trace \
      --xpath '/trace-toc/run[@number="1"]/data/table[@schema="time-profile"]' \
      --output time-profile.xml

Frames below are verbatim `name` attributes from that export. Blank entries in
the raw export are frames without a symbol (typically the mach message wait
inside the synchronous XPC round trip).

The full .trace file is ~4 MB and is not committed; it is attached to the
Feedback Assistant report. The exported hang table is in `hangs.xml`.

What this shows
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every blocked call is parked in AVFoundation's client-side session object
(`avas::client::SessionCore`), i.e. in the synchronous round trip to the audio
daemon. Not one of the samples is doing app-side work: the app is waiting.
Even read-only properties (`inputNumberOfChannels`, `outputNumberOfChannels`)
go through `GetProperty` on the same path and block for seconds.
